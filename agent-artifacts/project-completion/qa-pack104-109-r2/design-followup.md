# Narrow design follow-up: bounded first/last 100

**Recommendation: do not commission this R3 as a sufficient repair of the four-strong-options gate.** First-100 and circular last-100 buffers are realistic bounded-storage policies and stronger than zero-detail C. Both preserve the numerical minimum and budget, but the unchanged explicit requirement for representative coverage of the whole week defeats both by the same mechanism. They are two temporal directions of one error, not two independently competitive tradeoffs.

At 10,000,000 uniformly timed events/week, 100 consecutive records cover about six seconds; at 500/week, they cover about 1.4 days. Thus both siblings plainly fail the stated whole-week requirement, even though the rare sibling loses less coverage. Neither first nor last has a stated diagnostic advantage. 'Preserved all week' describes how long the first records remain available; it does not make their event times representative. A/B winners remain unique, but that alone does not establish four strong choices.

This proposal is a genuine improvement in feasibility over R2: all actions can be executed, C/D retain useful complete examples, and a learner must distinguish sample count from temporal representativeness. It could serve a deliberately simpler exercise on that distinction. Under the current stricter demand for four strong Middle alternatives in both frequency states, however, it remains an elimination exercise with two closely related coverage failures.

The family is intrinsically a two-way decision while the action space and objective fix full retention versus every-hundredth sampling, exact count, known sufficient minimum, unbiased sampling and all-week coverage. Additional options naturally become explicit constraint violations. To retain four strong actions, redesign the shared decision model with a source-grounded tradeoff that gives additional policies a meaningful benefit and requires comparison (for example, a specified diagnostic value for temporal strata or freshness against sample completeness). Such a redesign needs explicit blueprint/family review and may no longer be a narrow repair. Alternatively accept the two substantive alternatives or relax the four-strong-options requirement explicitly. No case or source change is approved by this note.
